Vietnamese Legal Ontology

VLOnto

An ontology and knowledge graph of Vietnamese law

VLOnto describes Vietnamese legal documents and their articles, the bodies that issue them, their validity and the dated events that replace, amend or repeal them, legal-basis and citation links, legal questions and answers, terminology and news. The knowledge graph instantiates the ontology from four public datasets and links every record back to its source.

Built from four public datasets

All four datasets are published on Hugging Face by TMQuan. VLOnto keeps metadata, structure, short labels and the links it derives; it does not republish document bodies, Q&A or news text, glossary definitions or person names; the only exception is the title of each question on the Traces page. Each record links to its source for the text.

What the ontology covers

47 classes, 46 object properties and 35 datatype properties in eleven modules, 13 SKOS concept schemes, and alignments with ELI, SKOS, PROV-O, schema.org, the W3C Organization ontology and DCTERMS. Every term has an English and a Vietnamese label.

Not legal advice

VLOnto is not an official legal text and is not legal advice. Relations between documents and the validity of documents are extracted automatically and may be wrong; validity is derived as of 2026-11-08 and is unknown for 28,660 documents. Read the document at its official source before relying on it, and consult a qualified lawyer for legal questions.
