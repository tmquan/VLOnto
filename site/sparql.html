Query the knowledge graph in your browser

Oxigraph runs as WebAssembly inside this page, so queries work offline and nothing leaves your computer. The default dataset is the core subset of the knowledge graph: the ontology plus every document of legal-force rank 0–2 (Constitution, codes, laws, National Assembly resolutions, ordinances and Standing Committee resolutions) with their change events, legal-basis links and citations among themselves.

Good to know

  • Documents are typed by their specific class (vlo:Law, vlo:Decree, …); there is no reasoner, so ?d a vlo:LegalDocument matches nothing. Use vlo:documentNumber or a specific class.
  • Shortcut properties such as vlo:replaces, vlo:amends and vlo:legalBasis link documents directly; vlo:LegalChange events carry the date and evidence.
  • Document IRIs in the results open the document in the 3D legal network.
  • Queries run on the main thread; add LIMIT to exploratory queries.
  • The full graph (31.5 million triples) is in the RDF dump listed on the downloads page.
