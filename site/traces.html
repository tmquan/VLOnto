How to read
  • The scene is the 3D legal network of the network page: every document at its place, its height the layer of its legal force. All 540 traced answers of the 27 areas live in it: each traced law is its document there, with its chapters, articles and clauses hanging just below it.
  • Under the lowest layer lie the answers, then the questions on a plane of their own, the terms they name in between and the other answers that cite the same provisions just under the answers. A question stands under the laws its trace reaches; an area's name marks the middle of its questions.
  • Everything is drawn at once and light; the chosen traces are solid. A click on a question (list or scene) chooses it alone; its round button, Space on the row or Ctrl/⌘-click add or remove it, and an area's “All 20” chooses its 20 questions. Dark strokes are text-verified (at least half of the cited provision's word 5-grams appear in the answer, as in the paper) and include the hop from the question's term to the clause that defines it; a thinner stroke is structural, kept and not verified.
  • Question titles are as published on thuvienphapluat.vn; English glosses and the normalised English law names are machine-assisted. Per area: the 20 articles its answers cite most, one representative answer each. No answer text, provision text, definitions or person names are shown; each node links to its source.

Paper: Vietnamese Legal Ontology: Every legal answer, traced to the article that grounds it (Bui & Tran Minh, 2026, arXiv:2611.11426). Numbers here are computed from the VLOnto v1 release.
