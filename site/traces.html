    How to read
    • One graph holds all 540 traced answers of the 27 areas; every law, provision, term and answer appears once, and shared ones link the areas that use them. Each area is a region of the floor, in the order of the tabs.
    • Within a region the laws hang from the top with their chapters, articles and clauses below them; questions and answers stand on the floor under the provisions they cite, the terms they name float between them, and the other answers that cite the same provisions hang faded beneath.
    • Pick a question: its trace lights up inside the whole graph. Dark strokes are text-verified (at least half of the cited provision's word 5-grams appear in the answer, as in the paper) and include the hop from the question's term to the clause that defines it; a thinner stroke is structural, kept and not verified.
    • Question titles are as published on thuvienphapluat.vn; English glosses and the normalised English law names are machine-assisted. Per area: the 20 articles its answers cite most, one representative answer each. No answer text, provision text, definitions or person names are shown; each node links to its source.

    Paper: Vietnamese Legal Ontology: Every legal answer, traced to the article that grounds it (Bui & Tran Minh, 2026, arXiv:2611.11426). Numbers here are computed from the VLOnto v1 release.
