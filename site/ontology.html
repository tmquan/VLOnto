The VLOnto ontology

An OWL 2 ontology of Vietnamese law in eleven modules. Namespace https://tmquan.github.io/VLOnto/ontology# (prefix vlo:), version 0.1.0, licence CC BY 4.0. Every class and property has English and Vietnamese labels, an English definition and a module annotation.

Alignments with external vocabularies

VLOnto terms are sub-classes or sub-properties of terms of established vocabularies, so tools that know ELI, SKOS, PROV-O, schema.org, ORG or DCTERMS can read the knowledge graph.
